Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Ikanisa ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Gikundiro mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you use the anonymous session or opt into certain features, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Optional display name and optional mobile money phone number
  • Membership Data: Fan club membership details, loyalty points
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, contribution records, ticket purchases

1.2 Device Information

We automatically collect device and app attestation identifiers for security purposes (Firebase App Check). If notifications are enabled, we may also collect a push messaging token for message delivery.

1.3 SMS Data (Optional)

Some supported deployments may include optional payment-message import features to help reconcile eligible mobile money transactions. Availability depends on the release channel, platform, and policy approvals for that deployment.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's features and services
  • To process transactions and manage your membership
  • To enable gamification features (predictions, leaderboards)
  • To send you match updates and club notifications (with your consent)
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience
  • To ensure security through Firebase App Check
  • To collect crash, analytics, or performance diagnostics only when the release configuration and applicable consent permit it

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Supabase: Our backend provider for secure data storage and authentication
  • Firebase: For app security verification (App Check), optional push messaging, and configuration/consent-gated crash, performance, and analytics diagnostics where enabled. These services can process installation identifiers, device/app information, crash traces, performance measurements, and privacy-filtered product-interaction events.

4. Data Security

  • All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Sensitive data is stored with encryption at rest
  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own data

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Transaction history: Retained for 3 years for legal compliance

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information via the App settings
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion through Settings > Delete Account
  • Withdraw Consent: Disable optional features such as SMS auto-read at any time

If you cannot access the App, request deletion from the public web by emailing support@ikanisa.com with the subject “Gikundiro account deletion request.” Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials, or identity documents. The support team must verify account control through an approved privacy-safe process before deletion. Transaction, fraud-prevention, security, and audit records may be retained only where lawful and under the approved retention schedule.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us:

  • Email: support@ikanisa.com
  • Website: gikundiro.com
