## Interpretation rules used by this package

- Marketing copy describes only behavior available in, or explicitly gated by,
  the release candidate. It does not promise live data, successful payment,
  ticket issuance, rewards, or official rights that have not been approved.
- Google Data Safety covers data transmitted off device by the app or bundled
  SDKs. Final answers must reflect the exact compiled feature flags and SDK
  configuration.
- Apple App Privacy includes the app and third-party partners and must be
  reconciled to the exact archive.
- Store policies, not internal naming, determine whether a purchase is a
  physical good, digital good, financial feature, contest, or gambling feature.
- User-selected names, notes, or other content visible to another user are
  treated conservatively as UGC until a release-specific assessment proves
  otherwise.
