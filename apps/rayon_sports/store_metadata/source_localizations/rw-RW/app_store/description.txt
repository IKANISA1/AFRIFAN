Gikundiro ihuriza hamwe amakuru ya Rayon Sports FC na serivisi zigenewe abafana muri porogaramu imwe.

AMAKURU Y'IKIPE N'IMIKINO
Reba amakuru yatangajwe n'ikipe, gahunda y'imikino, ibisubizo, urutonde n'ibindi bijyanye n'imikino. Amakuru y'ako kanya agaragara gusa iyo hari isoko yemewe y'amakuru.

AMATIKE
Reba imikino yatangajwe n'uburyo bw'amatike. Iyo uburyo bwo kwishyura bwemewe bukora, Gikundiro ishobora kukohereza kuri serivisi ya mobile money. Itike igaragara nyuma y'uko sisitemu ibishinzwe yemeje ubwishyu.

UBUNYAMURYANGO N'INKUNGA
Reba ibyiciro by'ubunyamuryango byatangajwe, ohereza ubusabe kandi urebe ubunyamuryango cyangwa inkunga byemejwe. Ibyemewe n'inyungu biterwa n'imiterere yemejwe n'ikipe.

IDUKA
Reba ibicuruzwa byatangajwe, tegura komande kandi ukoreshe uburyo bwo kwishyura bwemewe iyo buhari. Komande iguma itaremezwa kugeza sisitemu iyemeje.

IBIKORWA BY'ABAFANA
Jya mu mikino yo guteganya ibisubizo yatangajwe n'ikipe ku buntu kandi urebe amateka y'amanota wabonye iyo bihari. Verisiyo rusange nta marushanwa y'imbogamizi, jackpot, ibihembo, guhererekanya amanota, gushakisha abandi bafana cyangwa urutonde rw'abatsinze itanga. Gikundiro si porogaramu yo gutega amafaranga. Amanota ya FET ni amanota yo muri porogaramu gusa: nta gaciro k'amafaranga afite, ntashobora kugurwa cyangwa kubikuzwa kandi nta gihembo yizeza.

IBANGA N'UBUGENZUZI
Koresha konti y'umushyitsi cyangwa kwinjira na telefoni iyo bishoboka, hitamo niba wemera amatangazo kandi usabe gusiba konti unyuze ku mwirondoro wawe.

Imikorere iboneka iterwa na gahunda y'ikipe, imiterere ya serivisi n'uburyo bwemewe. Ibiciro, ububiko, amatike, ubunyamuryango n'ibisubizo biva muri sisitemu yemewe.

Gikundiro yakozwe na Ikanisa igenewe abafana ba Rayon Sports.
