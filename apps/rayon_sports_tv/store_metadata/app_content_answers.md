# Google Play App content working answers — Gikundiro TV

Status: draft only. Re-answer using the current Play Console wording for the
exact submitted AAB.

Distribution intent is a public Google Play TV listing. Internal testing is the
current pre-production stage; public promotion remains subject to accountable
distribution, audience, rights, privacy, device, language, and release-owner
approval.

## App access

- Restricted functionality: **Yes**. A display must be paired and assigned by an
  authorized owner before live content appears.
- Authentication in the TV app: **No supporter login**. The app provisions an
  anonymous device identity and later stores a revocable device credential.
- Reviewer path: use the controlled reviewer procedure in
  `reviewer_access.md`. Real codes and credentials belong only in Play Console.

## Ads

- Contains ads: **No**, based on source/package review.
- Reconfirm from the exact AAB and dependency inventory.

## Target audience and content

- Intended use: managed venue displays operated by adults.
- Not designed for children. Do not select child age groups without a separate
  Families-policy assessment and approval.
- Complete the current IARC questionnaire after the exact questions, standings,
  and public-alias content are approved.

## User-generated content

- The TV client accepts no participant text or entry codes.
- It can display public aliases and question/session content supplied by the
  backend. Confirm these values are system-generated or pre-moderated and that
  the mobile/service moderation controls satisfy Play policy before any public
  distribution.
- If unrestricted user-authored content can reach a public TV, the release is
  blocked until terms, filtering, reporting, blocking where applicable,
  moderation, and support operations are proven.

## Payments, contests, and financial features

- Purchases in the TV app: **No**.
- Payment controls or financial data display: **No**.
- The TV may display an approved question, score, or public-alias ranking. It
  must not display or promise cash value, wagering, purchasable points,
  withdrawals, reward claims, or real-world prizes unless separately classified,
  legally authorized, age/geo controlled, and accepted by Play.
- Financial features declaration: select only current applicable console
  categories; source review found no TV financial feature.

- Government app: **No**.
