# Gikundiro TV

Read-only venue display for approved AFRICLUB matchday and supporter experiences. After an owner pairs and assigns the display, the app shows the game lobby, current question and choices, answer reveal, public-alias standings, final result, completion, and privacy-safe recovery states. Pairing is owner-only and temporary; the public display never accepts participant entry codes or displays supporter identity, credentials, private balances, reward claims, or orders.

Category: Sports  
Audience: Managed venue devices; not designed for children  
Distribution: Managed Google Play/private channel  
Support and privacy URLs: must be supplied and verified in the distribution console before release.

Required artwork: Android TV banner, 720p/1080p/4K screenshots, app icon, pairing screen, live state, disconnected/retry state, and expired/revoked state. Screenshots must use non-production codes and contain no credentials.
