# AFRICLUB Mobile Design Contract

AFRICLUB Flutter fan apps use a MOBI/Revolut-style mobile UX benchmark as the
enforced direction. The benchmark applies to interaction model, visual system,
screen density, motion, state handling, accessibility, and repo governance.

This does not authorize false brand claims, Revolut impersonation, or replacing
Rayon Sports/AFRICLUB product facts. Club identity, crest assets, Supabase data,
tenant behavior, and owned product rules remain authoritative where the product
requires them.

Active design sources:

- `docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`
- `docs/design/MOBI_REVOLUT_100_PERCENT_ALIGNMENT_MATRIX.md`
- `docs/design.md` for legacy tooling and agents that still read that path
- `packages/core/core_theme` for Flutter tokens, typography, chrome, widgets,
  motion, and route-surface primitives

All dated recommendation-style design reports are superseded by the alignment
matrix. Do not revive old files, local screen styles, or non-enforcing
comparator language that weakens this contract.
