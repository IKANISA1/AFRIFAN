# Venue Staff Privacy Notice

AFRICLUB processes staff account identifiers, assigned club/venue, role, access status, and timestamped operational actions to authenticate staff, enforce venue isolation, protect supporters, investigate incidents, and meet financial/operational accountability obligations.

The venue portal must not display or export supporter contact details, private balances, authentication tokens, or unnecessary payment data. Staff audit events contain the operator identifier, venue scope, action, affected entity identifier, reason, and limited operational metadata. Access is limited to authorized club administrators and incident/privacy personnel.

The operational retention target is 12 months, subject to the approved legal retention schedule. Staff may request access or correction through the club privacy contact. Security concerns, incorrect assignments, or suspected account misuse must be reported immediately to the club administrator and privacy/security owner. Account deletion does not remove audit records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations; those records remain access-restricted and are deleted when the retention period ends.

Production deployment must replace this document’s ownership placeholders with named contacts and publish an accessible venue-staff notice URL before onboarding staff.
